THE CURRENT BLOCKER, IN DETAIL
------------------------------
Every self-built boot image on boot_b ends in fastboot after a fixed ~6 s. Measured so far:

  - diag stage 1 (log+console)            ~6 s
  - a park loop two instructions in       ~6 s
  - same park, stock cmdline              ~6 s
  - same park as header v2 + AVB footer   ~6 s
  - a park that deliberately resets after 30 s of counter spin: STILL ~6 s

A 30 s deliberate reset finishing in 6 s means the CPU never reaches our code: the 6 s is
constant no matter what the image does, so it is a host-side (ABL/XBL) timeout or refusal,
not a shim crash. Nothing is wasted by a failed run - boot_b restores in one command.

What was ruled OUT by control experiments:
  - stock kernel re-packed by our packer: does NOT boot (broke separately, see below)
  - untouched stock boot.img: BOOTS (18 s) -> the slot, partition and flash path are fine
  - header v1 vs v2: both fail identically
  - cmdline content (console=null vs stock): both fail
  - AVB footer presence: needed for the repack case, not sufficient for the shim
  - MMU/console/log code: a two-instruction image fails the same way

Still open: why ABL accepts (Sending/Writing OKAY) and then does not hand over. Next
experiments queued: (a) compare ABL's own log (/proc/last_kmsg, msm_rtb) after a failed
boot, (b) make the shim image *shape* identical to stock (43 MB kernel-sized payload,
v2, stock cmdline, stock footer) and shave it down from there, (c) test whether ABL
requires os_version/rollback fields to match the device, (d) test a payload known to be
executable (a tiny Linux Image) through our packer to separate "our packer" from "our code".

WHAT FAILED AND WHAT IT TAUGHT
------------------------------
- `fastboot boot` is unusable on this ABL: 4/4 attempts, both header versions, TWRP and shim.
  Flash instead. (docs/SHIM_BOOT.md)
- Slot a is unusable: OOS left no system metadata, so any kernel there lands in recovery.
  Experiments go to boot_b, restore = backup/64f70df8/boot_b.img.
- A payload-less shim resets itself after 20 s - a fastboot fallback is not proof of failure.
- The stock boot.img carries an AVB footer at the partition end; an image without one is
  accepted by fastboot and never run. The stock footer is unsigned (algorithm NONE).
- Our first repacks were TRUNCATED: the ramdisk's last partial page was dropped and the
  footer/vbmeta landed inside it. Page padding fixed that class of bug.
- /proc/cmdline order differs from the stored cmdline; truncating from /proc drops
  androidboot.* and bootdevice arguments. Copy the stored field, never /proc.
- Measuring "did it boot" by watching adb gives false positives from the previous boot:
  `adb kill-server` before every reboot test.
- A repacked stock kernel still does not boot while stock itself does - that gap is the
  live lead, not the shim code.


RESTORE / SAFETY
----------------
Active slot must stay b. One-command restore, then reboot:

    fastboot flash boot_b backup/64f70df8/boot_b.img
    fastboot reboot

If ABL flips to a after failures: `fastboot --set-active=b`.
Backups: backup/64f70df8/ (41 partitions, md5 manifest). Never touched: persist, modemst*,
fsg, xbl, abl, tz, all still original. Nothing has been destroyed except userdata (twice:
unlock and the OOS->LOS format; both expected).
